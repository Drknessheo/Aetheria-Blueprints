1.  **Preparation of the Guideway:**
    *   Cut the Teak Baseplate (Part A) to exactly 1200mm. Ensure the surface is perfectly flat. Use a hand plane to smooth out any warping.
    *   Route or chisel the 5mm deep rebate into the Left and Right Guidance Walls (Parts B & C). This groove will hold the track magnets flush.
2.  **Installing the Track Magnets:**
    *   Clean the routed grooved channels.
    *   Apply a thin layer of Araldite Epoxy.
    *   Lay the N52 block magnets down, **all pointing NORTH pole UP**. 
    *   *Aetheria's Warning:* Neodymium magnets are incredibly strong and brittle. Do not let them snap together, or they will shatter like glass. Keep them spaced apart until they are set in the epoxy.
3.  **Securing the Walls:**
    *   Attach the Guidance Walls to the Baseplate using the brass screws. Pre-drill the holes to prevent the Teak from splitting.
4.  **Building the Carriage:**

    *   Mount four N52 magnets on the underside corners of the carriage. **These magnets must have their NORTH poles pointing DOWN** to repel the track magnets.
